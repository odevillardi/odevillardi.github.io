I created this guitar that I call my <strong>JZero Stark</strong>.

It is a J-style mate black bodyshape with a single <a href="https://www.fbs-music.com/?page_id=738">FBS humbucker</a> and a StarCaster neck. I think it’s funny because, a few weeks after I finished it, Josh Scott revealed his custom <a href="https://biltinternational.com/products/jhs-relevator">Bilt Revelator</a> which is, I think, the exact opposite of my <em>JZero Stark</em>.

Or maybe I was inspired (counter inspired?) by seing it on numerous shows on <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@jhspedals">JHS Youtube channel</a>.
